The Hadramout Research Center and the Al-Mashqas Students Forum (Al-Raydah and Qusay'ar district) signed a cooperation agreement in the Red Hall at Al-Ahgaff University, Mukalla, to run training programs and courses in the Center's rooms for students from the eastern districts.

It was signed for the Center by Mohammed Salem Al-Bouri, Director of Training, and for the Forum by Dr. Saleh Aidha Al-Jumhi, the Forum's president.
