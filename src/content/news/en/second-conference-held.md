On Saturday 27 December 2025, Al-Ahgaff University, through the Hadramout Research Center, held its **Second International Scientific Conference** online via Zoom, under the title **"Hadramis in East Africa: Integration and Preservation of Identity"**.

Prof. Sami Al-Sharif, Secretary General of the League of Islamic Universities, addressed the opening session, highlighting the role Hadramis played in carrying Arab and Islamic culture and scholarship to East Africa.
