The preparatory committee for the Hadramout Research Center's Second Scientific Conference held its second meeting in Mukalla, chaired by Prof. Sadiq Omar Maknoon, committee chair, Vice-President of the University and Executive Director of the Center.

The conference is dedicated to Hadrami migration to East Africa and was set for 27–28 December 2025. The meeting reviewed and assessed the papers submitted and stressed drawing on local and international scholarly expertise.

The conference has since taken place — see the [conference page](/conferences/second-scientific-conference).
