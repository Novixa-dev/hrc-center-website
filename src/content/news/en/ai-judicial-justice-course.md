The Hadramout Court of Appeal and the Hadramout Research Center at Al-Ahgaff University launched a specialist course, **"Artificial Intelligence and Judicial Justice"**, for members of the judiciary in Hadramout governorate, running over three days (20–22 September 2026).

## What it covers

- Legal concepts and applications of AI in judicial work.
- Prompt engineering.
- Using AI tools to review and analyse case files within professional and legal safeguards.
- Applying AI to court administration and public services.

The course is delivered by Counsellor Dr. Hashem Alawi Muqaibel, a specialist in AI applications in legal and judicial settings.
