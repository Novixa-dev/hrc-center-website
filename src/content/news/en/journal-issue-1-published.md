The Hadramout Research Center at Al-Ahgaff University published **Issue 1** of the **Al-Ahgaff Journal of Social Sciences**, a peer-reviewed journal published twice a year.

The issue carries **seven research papers** on social and human questions, reviewed by senior professors of the social sciences and humanities in Yemen and abroad.

More about the journal and its issues on the [Research & Publishing](/research#journal) page.
