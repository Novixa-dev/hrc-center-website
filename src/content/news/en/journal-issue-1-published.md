In September 2024 the Hadramout Research Center published the first issue of the
**Al-Ahgaff Journal for Social Sciences**, a peer-reviewed scholarly periodical in the
social sciences.

More about the journal — aims, submissions, and the issue archive — is on the
[Research](/research#journal) page.
