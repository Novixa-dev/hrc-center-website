The preparatory committee for the Hadramout Research Center's Second Scientific Conference held its first meeting, chaired by Prof. Sadiq Omar Maknoon, committee chair and Executive Director of the Center.

The meeting discussed the conference's title and tracks. The conference is dedicated to Hadrami migration to East Africa, with scholarly presentations, research panels and cultural contributions.
