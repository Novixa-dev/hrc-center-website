The Hadramout Research Center held a graduation-research ceremony for the **12th cohort** of its **English Language Diploma** — **13 students** — in the training hall of the Al-Ahgaff University Presidency in Mukalla.

The ceremony was attended by Dr. Salem Salah Bajaber, Director General of the Ministry of Technical Education & Vocational Training office for coastal Hadramout; Mohammed Al-Bouri, the Center's Director of Training, and Counsellor Dr. Hashem Alawi Muqaibel addressed the graduates.
