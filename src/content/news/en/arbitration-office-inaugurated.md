On Saturday 10 October 2026, the Hadramout Research Center at Al-Ahgaff University opened its **International Office for Arbitration & Dispute Resolution** in Mukalla, alongside a ceremony for the **first cohort of the International Commercial Arbitration Diploma**.

## The diploma

- **25 participants** from Yemeni governorates and from abroad.
- **120 training hours**, accredited by the Ministry of Technical Education & Vocational Training.
- Graduates received their diploma certificates and an international-arbitrator card issued by the Center.

## What the Office is for

- Spreading a culture of arbitration and alternative means of settling commercial disputes.
- Developing legal and commercial practice in step with international arbitration.
- Continuing training programs that prepare qualified international commercial arbitrators.
- Supporting justice in the business environment.

The Office is headed by Counsellor Dr. Hashem Alawi Muqaibel. The opening was attended by Prof. Sadiq Omar Maknoon, Vice-President of the University and Executive Director of the Center, Judge Taha Omar Al-Haddar, President of the Hadramout Court of Appeal, and representatives of local government, universities, and the legal and business community.
