On Wednesday 2 February 2022, at the office of the President of Hadhramout University, the Hadramout Research Center at Al-Ahgaff University and Hadhramout University's Center for Human & Social Studies signed a **memorandum of understanding and cooperation**.

It was signed for the Hadramout Research Center by its Executive Director, Dr. Sadiq Omar Maknoon, and for the Center for Human & Social Studies by its Director General, Dr. Mohammed Abubakr Muqaibel.

## Areas of cooperation

- Coordinating seminars, conferences and panel discussions.
- Joint training courses.
- Exchanging information and news.
- Seeking support for joint projects.
