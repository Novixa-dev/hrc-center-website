The Hadramout Research Center at Al-Ahgaff University has published **Issue 3** of the **Al-Ahgaff Journal of Social Sciences**, a peer-reviewed, semi-annual journal of specialist research in the social sciences and humanities.

The issue brings together research on a range of social and human questions, prepared to established scholarly methods and reviewed by senior professors and researchers in the social sciences and humanities in Yemen and abroad.

More about the journal and its issues on the [Research & Publishing](/research#journal) page.
