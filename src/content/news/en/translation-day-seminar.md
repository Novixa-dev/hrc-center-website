To mark International Translation Day (30 September), the Hadramout Research Center at Al-Ahgaff University held a seminar, **"Written and Oral Translation: Its Present and Future, Between Reality and Aspiration"**, moderated by Prof. Sadiq Omar Maknoon, Vice-President of the University and Executive Director of the Center.

## Speakers

- Dr. Ahmad Suryana, sworn translator with Indonesia's Ministry of Law and Human Rights.
- Prof. Abdulhamid Al-Shuja'a, Professor of Applied Linguistics and Translation, Sana'a University.
- Prof. Hussein Habtoor, Professor of Linguistics and Translation Studies, Shabwah University.

The seminar discussed the human translator's role alongside AI tools, the challenges and prospects of digital translation, and how to prepare interpreters for academic conferences.
