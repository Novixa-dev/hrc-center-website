Issue 1 of the **Al-Ahgaff Journal of Social Sciences** appeared in September 2024 — the first of the Hadramout Research Center's periodicals.

It brings together researchers from Yemen and abroad — the United Arab Emirates, Turkey and India — with peer-reviewed papers in history, literature, management, education and logic. Papers are published in Arabic.

The full issue can be downloaded from the Al-Ahgaff University website.
