## What it covers

- **Foundations:** course goals, design in the job market, design principles, comparing mobile design apps and their interfaces.
- **Type and text:** installing fonts, gradients, background removal, text fill and reflection, embossed and engraved effects.
- **Projects:** invitations and business cards, avatars, posters, logos, infographics, brochures, menus, roll-up banners, certificates, CVs and mockups.
