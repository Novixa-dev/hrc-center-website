## About the course

In June 2023 the Center ran — **for the first time on the Hadramout coast** — a **Soroban mental-arithmetic trainer course** on the Japanese abacus (**level one**), with the Genius Mind Centre for mental arithmetic and intelligence games.

## Participants

19 teachers from several private schools in Mukalla.

## Trainer

Mohammed Mahfoudh Basubaih, holder of an international TOT training licence and director of the Genius Mind Centre.
