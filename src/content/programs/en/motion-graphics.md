## About the diploma

Two courses: **Adobe Illustrator** for designing the images and elements used in video, then **Adobe After Effects** for animating them and adding effects and sound. Taught by Ahed Babaeer; it starts once enough people have enrolled.

The certificate is **accredited by the Ministry of Technical Education & Vocational Training**.
