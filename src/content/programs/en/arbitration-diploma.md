## About the diploma

The diploma prepares qualified practitioners in international commercial arbitration and alternative dispute resolution. It is accredited by the **Ministry of Technical Education & Vocational Training**.

## The first cohort

- **25 participants** from Yemeni governorates and from abroad.
- **120 training hours**.
- Graduates received the diploma certificate and an international-arbitrator card issued by the Hadramout Research Center.

The first cohort graduated in Mukalla on 10 October 2026, alongside the [opening of the International Office for Arbitration & Dispute Resolution](/news/arbitration-office-inaugurated).

No date has been announced for the next cohort yet. Register your interest and the Center will contact you when enrolment opens.
