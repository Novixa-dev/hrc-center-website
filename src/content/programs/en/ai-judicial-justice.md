## About the course

A specialist course the Center ran with the **Hadramout Court of Appeal** for members of the judiciary in Hadramout governorate.

## What it covers

- Legal concepts and applications of AI in judicial work.
- Prompt engineering.
- Using AI tools to review and analyse case files within professional and legal safeguards.
- Applying AI to court administration: the court presidency and management, the registry, the secretariat, enforcement and public services.

## Lecturer

Counsellor Dr. Hashem Alawi Muqaibel, a specialist in AI applications in legal and judicial settings.
