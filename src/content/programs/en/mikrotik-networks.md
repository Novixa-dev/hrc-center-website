## Who it is for

Graduates in computing and IT, and third- and fourth-year students in those fields.

## What it covers

- Introduction to MikroTik and its configuration.
- Router setup through Winbox; Telnet and SSH; command-line configuration.
- Sharing an internet connection through a MikroTik router and creating user accounts.
- Ways to reset a MikroTik server.

## Trainer

Majid Muqaibel — MSc in Information Technology (Al-Ahgaff University), head of network administration at Hadhramout University's IT center, 10+ years' experience, Cisco CCNA and CCNP, MikroTik trainer.
