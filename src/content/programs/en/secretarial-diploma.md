## About the diploma

A **ministry-accredited** professional program covering computer components, applications and operating systems, mixing theory with hands-on practice and closing with a practical assessment.

## Recent cohorts

- **December 2023:** cohort 87 began with seventeen trainees, taught by Eng. Omar Salem Bahakam.
- **February 2025:** the diploma was completed for students of the [Al-Mashqas Students Forum](/news/secretarial-diploma-mashqas) under the cooperation agreement.
