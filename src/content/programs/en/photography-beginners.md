## What it covers

32 topics, including:

- The history and types of digital cameras and how they work; camera parts, settings and shooting modes.
- Composition: the rule of thirds, shot sizes, camera angles and lighting.
- Lenses, ISO, the exposure triangle, night and silhouette photography.
- Mobile photography, editing in Lightroom and CapCut, and product photography.

Aisha Baamran, professional photography trainer.
