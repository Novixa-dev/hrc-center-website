## About the program

The Intermediate Diploma in English is one of the longest-running programs of the Center's vocational, technical and professional training department, and closes with graduation projects that trainees present and defend.

## Recent cohorts

- **November 2023:** the Center celebrated the graduation of the [11th cohort](https://ahgaff.edu/centers/1/articles/%D9%85%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%B2-%D8%A8%D8%AD%D9%88%D8%AB-%D8%AD%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%85%D9%88%D8%AA-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%88-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AF%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8-%D9%88-%D8%AA%D9%86%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AC%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%B9-%D9%8A%D8%AD%D8%AA%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A8%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%B1%D8%AC-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%81%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D8%B4%D8%B1-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%A8%D9%84%D9%88%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%B3/) and its graduation projects.
- **January 2025:** 13 students of the [12th cohort](/news/english-diploma-graduation) presented their graduation research, attended by the Director General of the Ministry of Technical Education & Vocational Training office for coastal Hadramout.

## How to register

Cohort dates are announced on the Center's channels. To ask about the next cohort, [contact the Center](/contact).
