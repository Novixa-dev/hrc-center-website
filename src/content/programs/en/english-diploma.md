## About the program

The Intermediate English Diploma is a program of the Center's Vocational, Technical and
Professional Training department. It focuses on listening, speaking, reading and writing,
combining classroom instruction with practice.

## Who it's for

Students, employees and anyone looking to strengthen their English for study or work.

## How to enrol

Intake dates are announced on the Center's channels. To ask about the next intake,
contact the Center.
