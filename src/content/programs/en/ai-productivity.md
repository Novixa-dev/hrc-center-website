## About the course

A practical course open to anyone interested, starting once enough people have enrolled, taught by Ahed Babaeer.

## What it covers

- Key AI tools and how to use them; working smarter with intelligent tools.
- Prompt engineering.
- Designing social posts, logos and mockups; creating, enhancing and upscaling professional images.
- Summarising research for university students.
- Text-to-speech, text-to-song and voice cloning.
- Scripts for ads, motion graphics and scenarios; text- or image-to-video.
