## About the conference

The Hadramout Research Center devoted its second scientific conference to **Hadrami migration to East Africa**, under the title **"Hadramis in East Africa: Integration and Preservation of Identity"**. As its title says, it examines how Hadrami migrants integrated into East African societies while preserving their identity.

## How it came together

- **January 2024:** the preparatory committee, chaired by Prof. Sadiq Omar Maknoon, held its first meeting on the conference title and tracks.
- **September 2025:** at its second meeting the committee reviewed and assessed the submitted papers, and the dates were set.
- **27 December 2025:** the conference was held online via Zoom.

## Opening session

Prof. Sami Al-Sharif, Secretary General of the League of Islamic Universities, addressed the opening, highlighting the early and lasting role of Hadramis in carrying Arab and Islamic culture and scholarship to East Africa — Kenya, Somalia, Tanzania and the Comoros.
