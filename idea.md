## 2. Background & Research

### 2.1 Al-Ahgaff University

Al-Ahgaff University is a **non-profit private university** established in **1994** in Hadramout, Yemen. It was the **first university established in Hadramout Governorate**. The university operates under Ministerial Decree No. (5) issued on February 8, 1994.

**Key Facts:**
- **Location:** Fuah – Almasaken, Mukalla, Hadramout
- **Telephone:** 00967-361288
- **President:** Professor Abdullah Mohammed Baharoon
- **Vice President:** Professor Sadiq Omar Maknoon
- **Faculties:** 6 colleges including Sharia & Law, Management & Economics, Computer Science & Engineering, Arts & Humanities, Women's Studies, and Graduate Studies
- **Type:** Private, charitable
- **Website:** https://ahgaff.edu

### 2.2 Hadramout Research Center (HRC)

The Hadramout Research Center is a specialized research and training center operating under Al-Ahgaff University.

**Key Facts:**
- **Established:** December 2013
- **Location:** Third floor, Al-Ahgaff University Presidency Building, Fuah – Almasaken, Mukalla
- **Legal Status:** Independent financial and administrative entity under Al-Ahgaff University
- **Chairman of Board of Trustees:** Muhammad Mbarak Bin-Dohry
- **Executive Director:** Professor Sadiq Omar Maknoon

**Core Activities:**
1. Research and studies
2. Consultations (legal, social, financial, technological)
3. Arbitration services
4. Training and capacity building
5. Translation services
6. Conferences and seminars
7. Publishing (Al-Ahgaff Journal of Social Sciences)

**Training Programs:**
- Intermediate Diploma in English
- Accounting courses
- Economics courses
- Montage and editing
- Design courses
- Computer courses (Soroban)
