# Brand Identity & Voice — FINAL (Sprint 1)

> Voice + positioning signed off S1-T08. Visual identity is final **for the logo‑light
> interim**; HRC's own logo (checklist I1), when provided, may shift the mark and colours
> — those points are flagged and the swap is a small, contained change.

## 1. Brand essence

**A knowledge beacon for Hadramout.** HRC turns Hadramout's questions — social, economic,
legal, cultural — into research, counsel and skills, in service of the region's
development, under the wing of its first university.

- **Personality:** scholarly, grounded, generous, precise, unhurried.
- **Not:** flashy, corporate, salesy, trend‑chasing.

## 2. Positioning statement

> For students, researchers and organizations in Hadramout, the Hadramout Research Center
> is the University of Al‑Ahgaff's arm for research, consultancy and training — a credible,
> local institution that illuminates Hadramout's past, present and future.

## 3. Relationship to Al‑Ahgaff University

- HRC is visually **its own identity** (navy + bronze, its own mark) but always
  **clearly affiliated**.
- Affiliation shown as a lockup: HRC mark + "A center of Al‑Ahgaff University" /
  "أحد مراكز جامعة الأحقاف", linking to `ahgaff.edu`. Placed in the footer (always) and
  optionally a slim top strip.
- Mirror the university's three‑pillar language (education / research / community).

## 4. Verbal identity

### Voice attributes

| We are… | So we… | We avoid… |
|---|---|---|
| Scholarly | use precise nouns, cite, define terms | jargon for its own sake, vagueness |
| Grounded | name Hadramout, name real programs and people | generic "innovation" talk |
| Generous | explain how to engage, lower barriers | gatekeeping tone, walls of prose |
| Measured | state facts plainly, one idea per sentence | hype, exclamation marks, superlatives |
| Bilingual‑native | write AR first as real Arabic; localize EN as real English | machine‑translation cadence |

### Tone by context

- **Headlines:** confident, concrete, short. ("برامج تدريبية لسوق حضرموت" / "Training built
  for Hadramout's economy.")
- **Body:** third person for the institution; explanatory; scannable.
- **CTAs / forms:** second person, warm, action‑named ("اطلب استشارة" / "Request a consultation").
- **Errors / empty states:** plain, helpful, no blame.

### Boilerplate (draft — confirm facts)

> **AR:** مركز بحوث حضرموت مركزٌ علميٌّ تابعٌ لجامعة الأحقاف، تأسس عام 2013، يعمل في البحث
> والدراسات والاستشارات والتدريب وخدمة المجتمع في حضرموت، وله استقلالٌ ماليٌّ وإداري.
>
> **EN:** The Hadramout Research Center is a scholarly center of Al‑Ahgaff University,
> established in 2013, working in research, consultancy, training and community service
> across Hadramout, with financial and administrative independence.

## 5. Visual identity (draft)

- **Wordmark:** until HRC provides one — a typographic lockup: "مركز بحوث حضرموت" in the
  chosen Naskh display face, with "Hadramout Research Center" set smaller in EB Garamond;
  an optional geometric mark.
- **Geometric motif — chosen:** the **eight‑point star (نجمة)** from Hadrami carved doors
  and window screens. Single stroke weight, one colour, no fill + centre dot. Used only in
  the header mark, favicon, OG background, and once in the footer — never as a bullet or a
  repeated divider. Full spec: [`08-og-and-brand-assets.md`](08-og-and-brand-assets.md) §1.
  (Considered and rejected: a stepped mudbrick‑arch silhouette — too close to a generic
  "building" icon; a manuscript‑corner ornament — too fussy at small sizes.)
- **Color:** navy `#1E3A5F` + bronze `#A16207` (see design brief §4).
- **Photography treatment:** duotone (navy) or clean full‑color with consistent warmth;
  captions always; people credited/consented.
- **Clear space & sizing:** defined once the real mark exists.

## 7. Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Lead with the mandate and concrete offerings | Lead with a hero slider of stock images |
| Use the bronze accent only for the one primary action per view | Rainbow of accent colors |
| Show governance and affiliation (trust) | Hide who runs the Center |
| Write Arabic that a Hadrami academic would nod at | Publish literal EN→AR translation |
| Keep motion subtle and meaningful | Animate everything on scroll |
| Use the motif sparingly | Wallpaper the site in geometry |
