Colour

Institutional navy, an umber accent, cool paper

Navy does the work; a deep umber‑bronze marks the single most important action in any view. Neutrals carry a faint navy bias so they read as chosen. Every pairing below is measured against WCAG 2.2 — ratios shown.

The bronze rule. #A16207 reads at 4.9:1 on white — fine for fills and 24 px+ graphics, marginal for text. So the text, link and CTA colour is the deeper #854D0E (6.9:1), and hairline dividers (#CBD5E1) stay decorative while anything you interact with gets the stronger #64748B edge.

Typography

Two calligraphic pairs, one system

Arabic leads. Amiri — a revival of the Būlāq/Amiria Press Naskh — carries the display voice with the gravitas a Hadrami institution earns; IBM Plex Sans Arabic keeps running text modern and legible. On the Latin side EB Garamond shares Amiri's old‑style, pen‑drawn axis; Inter handles UI. Numerals and coordinates use IBM Plex Mono, tying back to the Arabic body face.

Motion

Quiet, and it means something

Motion orients and confirms — it never delays reading. Sections rise a short distance into place on first view; interactive elements give instant feedback. Everything below is disabled the moment the reader asks for reduced motion.
