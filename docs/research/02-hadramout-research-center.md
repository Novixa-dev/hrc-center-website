## Services (for the Services page)

Consolidated from both center pages + client brief:

1. **Scientific research & studies** — commissioned and self‑initiated research on
   Hadramout's society, economy, heritage, law.
2. **Consultations** — financial, economic, social, legal, technological / technical.
3. **Arbitration** — dispute resolution incl. international arbitration and
   dispute‑settlement training. 🟡
4. **Training & capacity building** — professional, technical and vocational courses
   and diplomas (see below).
5. **Translation & linguistic editing** — translation and Arabic proofreading services. 🟡
6. **Conferences, seminars & scientific workshops** — organizing and hosting academic events.
7. **Publishing** — books and the *Al‑Ahgaff Journal for Social Sciences*; printing and
   scholarly publication support.

### Arbitration

- **2026-10-10 — International Office for Arbitration & Dispute Resolution** inaugurated in Mukalla, with the
  graduation of the **first cohort of the International Commercial Arbitration Diploma**: 25 participants (from
  Yemeni governorates and abroad), **120 training hours**, accredited by the Ministry of Technical Education &
  Vocational Training; graduates received the diploma and an international-arbitrator card issued by HRC.
  Office aims: spread arbitration culture & alternative dispute resolution; develop legal/commercial practice;
  train qualified arbitrators; support justice in the business environment. ✅ [source](https://ahgaff.edu/centers/1/articles/%D9%85%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%B2-%D8%A8%D8%AD%D9%88%D8%AB-%D8%AD%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%85%D9%88%D8%AA-%D8%A8%D8%AC%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%AD%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%81-%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%B4%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%A8-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%88%D9%84%D9%8A-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%8A%D9%85-%D9%88%D8%AA%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B2%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%83%D9%84%D8%A7/)
