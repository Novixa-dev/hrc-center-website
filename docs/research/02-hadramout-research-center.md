## Services (for the Services page)

Consolidated from both center pages + client brief:

1. **Scientific research & studies** — commissioned and self‑initiated research on
   Hadramout's society, economy, heritage, law.
2. **Consultations** — financial, economic, social, legal, technological / technical.
3. **Arbitration** — dispute resolution incl. international arbitration and
   dispute‑settlement training. 🟡
4. **Training & capacity building** — professional, technical and vocational courses
   and diplomas (see below).
5. **Translation & linguistic editing** — translation and Arabic proofreading services. 🟡
6. **Conferences, seminars & scientific workshops** — organizing and hosting academic events.
7. **Publishing** — books and the *Al‑Ahgaff Journal for Social Sciences*; printing and
   scholarly publication support.
