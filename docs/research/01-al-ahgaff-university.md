## Implications for the HRC site

1. Add a persistent, subtle "Affiliated with Al‑Ahgaff University" signal (header or footer)
   linking to `ahgaff.edu`.
2. About page should tell the lineage story: university founded 1994, first in Hadramout,
   charitable model → HRC established 2013 as its research + community arm.
3. Mirror the three‑pillar language (education / research / community) so the center reads
   as an authentic extension of the university mission.
4. Don't copy university navigation or layout — HRC needs its own identity.
