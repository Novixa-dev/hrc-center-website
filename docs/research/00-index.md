## Confidence legend

Used throughout the research files:

- ✅ **Confirmed** — stated on an official HRC / Al‑Ahgaff source.
- 🟡 **Reported** — from a secondary source (Wikipedia, directory, press) or an older
  version of the site; plausible but confirm before publishing.
- 🔴 **Assumption / inference** — reasoned from context; must be confirmed by HRC.
- ❓ **Unknown** — needed for the site, not found anywhere.
