# Home — content brief & copy

**Goal:** In one screen, tell a visitor what HRC is, what it does (three pillars), and give
them a path to act. Then surface featured programs, the journal, and news.
**Primary CTA:** "Explore programs" (secondary: "Contact the Center").
**SEO title (≤60):** AR `مركز بحوث حضرموت — البحث والاستشارات والتدريب في حضرموت` ·
EN `Hadramout Research Center — Research, Consultancy & Training in Hadramout`
**Meta description (≤155):** the Center definition + "a center of Al‑Ahgaff University, est. 2013".
**JSON‑LD:** `EducationalOrganization` + `WebSite` (both already global in the layout).

Facts used: `docs/research/02` (definition, vision, departments), `docs/research/01`
(university lineage). Flags below → `content-review-checklist.md`.
