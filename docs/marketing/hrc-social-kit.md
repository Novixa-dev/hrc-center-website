# HRC Digital Launch Social Media Kit

> Comprehensive launch copy, channel strategies, and visual asset specifications for announcing the new Hadramout Research Center digital platform.

---

## 1. Launch Strategy & Channels

The launch campaign is structured across three primary channels:
- **Facebook:** Primary institutional channel reaching university leadership, researchers, academics in Yemen, and community partners in Hadramout.
- **Instagram:** Visual engagement reaching university students, trainees in vocational and language diplomas, and the broader community.
- **LinkedIn / Academic Networks:** Professional outreach targeting peer research institutions, regional think tanks, and international scholars.

## 4. Visual Asset Specifications

To maintain a consistent, high-prestige aesthetic across platforms, creative teams should adhere to the following specifications:

| Format | Dimensions | Usage | Graphic Guidelines |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Feed Square** | 1080 × 1080 px (1:1) | Instagram & Facebook feed | Clean serif typography, Hadrami eight-point star watermark, high-contrast dark navy or slate background |
| **Story / Reel** | 1080 × 1920 px (9:16) | Instagram / WhatsApp Status | Vertical layout, bottom 250px clear for interactive stickers |
| **Open Graph Card** | 1200 × 630 px (1.91:1) | Social link preview sharing | Automatically generated by the website server at `/[locale]/opengraph-image` |
| **Hero Graphic** | 1920 × 1080 px (16:9) | Facebook Page Banner | Al-Ahgaff University presidency building architectural line art + HRC brand typography |

### Brand Colors for Graphic Production
- **Deep Navy:** `#1e3a5f` (Primary brand color)
- **Hadrami Bronze / Sand:** `#854d0e` / `#c7861b` (Accent color)
- **Warm Paper:** `#f8fafc` (Backgrounds)
- **Pure White:** `#ffffff` (Text on dark cards)
