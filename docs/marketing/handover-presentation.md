## Slide 3: Cultural Identity & Craft
### الهوية البصرية الحضرمية الرصينة
- **The Hadrami Star (نجمة حضرموت):** An eight-point architectural geometric mark derived from Hadramout's palace and mud-brick architecture, symbolizing guidance and illumination.
- **Scholarly Typography:**
  - Headings: Classical academic serifs (`Amiri` in Arabic, `EB Garamond` in English) reflecting dignity and institutional stature.
  - Body Text: Modern, legible type (`IBM Plex Sans Arabic` and `Inter`).
- **Academic Palette:** Warm paper background (`#f8fafc`), deep institutional indigo (`#1e3a5f`), and warm sand/bronze accents (`#854d0e`).
- **Zero "AI-Smell":** Rejection of generic modern corporate templates, neon gradients, and floating bubbles.

## Slide 5: The "Institutional Truth" Standard
### سياسة النزاهة المؤسسية ودقة البيانات
- **Principle:** As an academic research institution, HRC must never publish unverified names, speculative dates, or unconfirmed prices.
- **Technical Gating:** Any data point awaiting formal appointment decrees (such as board members or specific event dates) is safely gated behind `confirmed: false` or rendered with neutral "Appointment announced soon" indicators.
- **The Content Review Checklist (`docs/content/content-review-checklist.md`):** A transparent 36-point register provided to HRC leadership to systematically review and supply official documentation.
